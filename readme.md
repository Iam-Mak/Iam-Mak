## About Me

My industry experience includes enterprise data integration, API-based data ingestion, and deployment automation. At Cubastion Consulting, I worked on integrating Azure Databricks with Salesforce Data Cloud, developing high-volume data ingestion solutions, and automating application releases across development, testing, and production environments.

I'm now applying that experience to hands-on data platform engineering projects, focusing on data processing, infrastructure provisioning, governance, access control, monitoring, and deployment. My aim is to understand not just how to build a solution, but also how to make it reliable, maintainable, and easier to operate.

I hold an MSc in Mathematical Modelling and Machine Learning from University College Cork. This gives me an additional foundation in statistical modelling and applied machine learning, complementing my primary focus on data platforms and cloud engineering.
